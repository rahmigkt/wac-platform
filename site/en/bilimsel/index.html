05OFFICIAL SYMPOSIA
01 / OFFICIAL SYMPOSIUM TITLES

All five symposia are represented at every congress.

01

Aerospace Sciences and Engineering

Aerodynamics, flight science, systems engineering and aircraft technologies.

02

Space Sciences and Technologies

Satellite systems, access to space, space research and mission technologies.

03

Aviation Management, Economics and Law

Airlines, airports, management, economics, regulation and law.

04

Aerospace Ecosystem

Institutions, industry, supply networks, collaboration and research ecosystems.

05

Aviation History and Society

Scientific history, cultural heritage, archives and social impact of aerospace.

02 / ACADEMIC PROCESS

From abstract call to proceedings.

01

Call for abstracts

Issued by the Academic Secretariat.

02

Peer review

At least two reviewers appointed by the Scientific Committee assess submissions.

03

Acceptance and presentation

Acceptance follows scientific review; oral or poster formats will be defined in the congress programme.

04

Proceedings publication

Following scientific review, accepted contributions are included in the congress proceedings.
