01 / WHY WAC?

A permanent link between research, industry and nations.

WAC is conceived as an international meeting ground for civil aviation, military aviation and defence technologies, and space. It aims to connect scientific knowledge with shared technologies and enduring cooperation.

The founding congress aims to establish delegations, research and industry networks, publications and reporting that can continue between congress meetings.

02 / THREE FIELDS

Three fields with shared technologies.

01

Civil aviation

Airlines, airports, traffic management, flight safety, sustainability and future aircraft.

02

Military aviation and defence technologies

Uncrewed systems, avionics, propulsion, advanced materials, dual-use technologies and autonomy.

03

Space

Satellites, communication, launch systems, science missions, space economy and law.

03 / CONGRESS PROGRAMME

Four distinct programme components.

01

Scientific symposia

Five official symposia with peer-reviewed contributions.

02

Global Aerospace Forum

Strategic dialogue, moderated panels and Q&A.

03

Aerospace Expo

An industry component with a separate participation and contracting process.

04

Cultural Programme

Optional exhibitions, ceremonies and public activities subject to host-country decisions.
