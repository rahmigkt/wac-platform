01 / DELEGATION STATUS

How a country gains institutional representation.

A legally recognised aerospace-related association, foundation, agency, professional body or authority can apply with evidence of representative capacity and acceptance of WAC's principles. Academic coordination from Türkiye does not automatically confer delegation status.

02 / APPLICATION DETAILS

Information specified by Article 5.

01

Institutional details

Full registered name, establishment date, legal status and country.

02

Representative and contact

Authorised representative and contact details.

03

Programme interests

Relevant symposium and forum themes.

04

Proposals

Optional experts, researchers, panel or symposium proposals.

Application channel

Applications will be received through the online form or authorised channel announced by the Delegation Office. Dates and contact information will be published in this section.

03 / ASSESSMENT

A four-step assessment and acceptance process.

01

Institutional application

Application with legal-status and representation documents.

02

Preliminary review

Delegation Office assesses track record, expertise, stability and cooperation.

03

Executive Board decision

Approval, additional-document request or refusal.

04

Written notification

Decision notified in writing; status begins on notification.

04 / RIGHTS AND DUTIES

Representation carries responsibilities.

01

Rights

One vote in the General Assembly, host-country selection vote, proposals for experts and sessions.

02

Responsibilities

Observe General Assembly decisions, update contacts and provide reasonable annual activity information upon request.

03

End of status

Voluntary withdrawal is possible; removal for breach provides for a two-thirds vote and a right of defence.
