A shared conversation about what comes next.

Strategic dialogue bringing together researchers, industry and regulatory or policy participants.

01 / SESSION DESIGN

Three components in each forum session.

01

Opening address

Framing the principal topic and questions.

02

Moderated panel

Balancing academic, industry and regulatory/policy perspectives.

03

Questions and answers

Direct exchange between speakers and participants.

02 / AREAS OF DIALOGUE

The institutional implications of scientific progress.

01

Transformation of civil aviation

New flight technologies, sustainability and aviation management.

02

Space and common infrastructure

Satellite services, access to space and emerging space economy.

03

Shared technology and governance

AI, autonomy, law, safety and regulation.

Proposed discussion themes

These themes derive from the project's thematic framework; they are not an announced panel schedule.

03 / FORUM ORGANISATION

Parallel tracks within one international forum.

The host-country Congress Organising Committee determines the number of parallel forum areas. Sessions seek balance between research, industry and policy perspectives.

Detailed programme

Speakers and session times will be announced in the detailed forum programme. Forum participation is separate from academic abstract submission.
