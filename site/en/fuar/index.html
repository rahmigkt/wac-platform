04 — AEROSPACE EXPO

A meeting ground for aerospace technology.

An independent industry expo, distinct from the scientific symposia, for manufacturers, technology organisations and institutional participants.

01 / INDEPENDENT INDUSTRY PROGRAMME

An independent pathway alongside scientific work.

The Aerospace Expo is an independent programme component alongside the scientific symposia and the Global Aerospace Forum. It is conceived for manufacturers, airlines, engineering and technology organisations, public institutions, and system suppliers.
