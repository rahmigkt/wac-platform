EXHIBITIONS / CULTURE

Exploring aerospace through science and culture.

Alongside the congress programme, WAC 2027 envisages exhibitions and public-facing activities connecting aerospace research, design, scientific history and society.

Programme concept ↗Institutional cooperation ↗
01 / PROGRAMME CONCEPT

Scientific ideas, tangible experiences.

The exhibition programme is conceived as a distinct complement to scientific sessions, the Global Aerospace Forum and the industry Expo. The ambition is to make complex ideas accessible through objects, models, artistic interpretations and hands-on examples.

01

History of science

Instruments, methods and concepts that shaped flight and the exploration of space.

02

Engineering and design

Geometric systems, materials, models and the process of transforming an idea into a working structure.

03

Public engagement

Accessible talks and exhibition formats connecting research and everyday life.

02 / PARTICIPATION

Programme details will be announced.

Individual exhibitions, participants, venues and visiting schedules are not yet confirmed. Cultural programming and industry exhibition stands follow separate planning and participation processes.
