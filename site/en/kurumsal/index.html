08 — WAC INSTITUTIONAL STRUCTURE

A lasting institution with roots in Türkiye.

A proposed international framework built around a General Assembly, national delegations, scientific coordination and founding principles.

Founding principles ↗
07Founding principles
01 / INSTITUTIONAL MODEL

From a congress to a lasting international institution.

WAC is conceived as a continuing platform connecting national delegations, research and industry beyond the triennial congress. The framework envisages decisions on independent legal personality at the founding congress.

Founding process

The institutional structure and legal framework of WAC will be addressed through the relevant founding congress decisions.

02 / FUNCTIONS

Separate responsibilities within a shared structure.

01

General Assembly

Delegation representation, strategic decisions and future hosting.

02

Management and oversight

Work between congresses, financial review and institutional continuity.

03

Academic coordination

Turkish Society for History of Science (established 1989), the envisaged academic coordinating institution.

04

Congress Organising Committee

Local congress delivery, implementation and financial responsibility.

03 / PRINCIPLES

Seven principles from the Founding Declaration.

01

Universality

Open participation to countries worldwide.

02

Independence

Independence from any single person, country or institution.

03

Scientific integrity

Academic freedom and peer review.

04

Cooperation

Collaboration across research, industry and policy.

05

Continuity

Institutional memory and work between congresses.

06

Transparency

Transparency in decisions and finance.

07

Sustainability

Environmental and social responsibility.

04 / FOUNDING PERIOD

Academic coordination, delegations and continuity.

The Turkish Society for History of Science plays a role in academic coordination and the founding period's administrative work. This does not confer automatic delegation status on Türkiye; each country's application is assessed separately.
