National delegation application

Applications from legally recognised organisations with aerospace-related representative capacity.

  • Applications include the institution's name, founding date, legal status and authorised representative's contacts.

Hosting candidacy for future congresses

Host-country nominations through recognised national delegations from the fourth congress onward.
