PLANNED DATES · 27–29 OCTOBER 2027 · ISTANBUL

A shared agenda for aviation and space.

The World Aerospace Congress aims to bring researchers, academic institutions, the aerospace industry and national delegations together in Istanbul. Scientific research, technology and international cooperation are at the heart of the three-day programme.

WAC / INTERNATIONAL SCIENTIFIC PLATFORM

Beyond a single event,
a lasting network.

The World Aerospace Congress is designed to convene researchers, academic institutions, industry and national delegations on a recurring basis. The years between congresses are part of the platform’s working cycle.
